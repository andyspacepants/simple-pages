Conclusion

The hard part isn't finishing. It's arriving.

Fitness, it turns out, is patient work — the product of months of easy days, well-timed recovery, and the humility to step back when the body says to. That's the race behind the race. Three years into this project, something has finally clicked.

There's still training to do. Taper carries its own risks. Race day has a way of writing its own ending. But on April 21, 2026 — six weeks from the starting line — this is the strongest, healthiest, and most-prepared I've been heading into any ultra.
